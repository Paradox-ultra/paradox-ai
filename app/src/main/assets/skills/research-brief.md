# Research Brief Skill

## Overview
Conducts structured research investigations, synthesizing findings from web sources, uploaded documents, or agentic browser crawls.

## Structure
1. **Background & Context**: Problem statement and historical context.
2. **Current State of the Art**: Survey of leading approaches or market offerings.
3. **Comparative Analysis**: Pros, cons, costs, and feasibility matrix.
4. **Conclusion & Recommendations**: Strategic recommendations based on evidence.

## Guidelines
- Never hallucinate unverified citations.
- Explicitly label preliminary vs confirmed data.
- Can synthesize findings directly into downloadable PDF reports or PPTX slide decks.
