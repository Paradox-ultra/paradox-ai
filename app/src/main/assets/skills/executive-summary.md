# Executive Summary Skill

## Overview
Synthesizes lengthy transcripts, articles, documents, or data dumps into concise, high-impact executive summaries.

## Structure
1. **Core Thesis / Objective**: 1-2 sentence bottom line up front (BLUF).
2. **Key Findings**: Top 3-5 critical insights with quantitative context where available.
3. **Risks & Tradeoffs**: Unresolved blockers or potential hazards.
4. **Immediate Next Steps**: Concrete owners and timelines.

## Guidelines
- Highlight critical metrics and dates.
- Avoid unnecessary jargon; write for decision-makers.
- Offer export to a formal PDF briefing memo via `create_pdf` or presentation deck via `create_pptx`.
